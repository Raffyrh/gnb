REG // 001 ACTIVE REGIMENT
LINE INFANTRY Standard Napoleonic Issue

DESCRIPTION

Infantry (a.k.a. Private, Marine, Trooper) is an offensive class specializing in overall versatility. It acts as the general, multi-purpose class of the game. Infantry are issued a Musket and a Sabre.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Musket with Bayonet, Sabre

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Pikeman/Lancer — trades Musket for a Pike with high melee range and damage. Cuirassier — focuses on withstanding harsh situations with damage resistance.

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Infantry are primarily encouraged to be on the frontlines, providing the best overall damage output of all classes. The default Musket boasts near-perfect accuracy at almost any range, incredibly high damage, and high penetration. Best for dealing with specialized/high-priority zombie types like Bombers.

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    Switch to Bayonet or Pike during an Officer's Charge for a massive 10x damage increase.

  • •

    Stay near an Officer for critical movement speed buffs during tight holdouts.

  • •

    The Carbine is great for run-and-gun playstyles without suffering from the reload speed debuff.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Infantry was one of the original 5 classes of Guts & Blackpowder. The specific rank name varies by regiment — called a Marine in Naval regiments, and Trooper in Cavalry divisions.
