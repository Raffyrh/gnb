OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

Infantry (a.k.a. Private, Marine, Trooper) is an offensive class specializing in overall versatility. It acts as the general, multi-purpose class of the game. Infantry are issued a Musket and a Sabre.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Infantry are primarily encouraged to be on the frontlines, providing the best overall damage output of all classes. The default Musket boasts near-perfect accuracy at almost any range, incredibly high damage, and high penetration.

REGIMENTAL BRANCHES

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Pikeman/Lancer — trades Musket for a Pike with high melee range and damage. Cuirassier — focuses on withstanding harsh situations with damage resistance.

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Switch to Bayonet or Pike during an Officer's Charge for a massive 10x damage increase.

  • 02

    Stay near an Officer for critical movement speed buffs during tight holdouts.

  • 03

    The Carbine is great for run-and-gun playstyles without the reload speed debuff.

WAR DISPATCH ARCHIVE

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Infantry was one of the original 5 classes of Guts & Blackpowder. The rank name varies by regiment — called Marine in Naval regiments, and Trooper in Cavalry divisions.
