Class Details

SAPPER

SUPPORT CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 003

"After some losses, the Russian sappers were able to break in the barricaded door; the soldiers rushed in."

— Adjutant-General August Ludwig von Nositz

DESCRIPTION

The Sapper is a support class specializing in defensive fortifications. By default, Sappers are issued an Axe and a Hammer.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Build Hammer, Heavy Axe

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Siege Engineer — uses Siege Engineer Armour for 66% damage resistance from physical attacks and 50% explosive damage resistance, at the cost of slower construction and movement.

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Very versatile in positioning. Best suited for defensive support during horde segments. The Axe boasts incredibly high damage and penetration compared to the Sabre. Defensive fortifications allow teammates time to reload and hinder zombie movement.

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    Build with the team's betterment in mind. Keep exits clear so you don't trap survivors.

  • •

    Bombers destroy clustered fortifications instantly — separate your lines to avoid cascade failure.

  • •

    You can deploy a maximum of 30 buildings per map, and 10 per Sapper.

  • •

    Always repair fortifications during wave intermissions. Build near Musicians for Hammer speed boost.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Sappers were traditionally recruited from the strongest, bravest men — specifically from Grenadier companies. Sappers were permitted not to shave to save time on campaigns. Originally, Marines could not be Sappers, but this was added in the Roscoff Update.
