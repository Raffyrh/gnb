SAPPER

SUPPORT CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 003

"After some losses, the Russian sappers were able to break in the barricaded door; the soldiers rushed in."

— Adjutant-General August Ludwig von Nositz
REG // 003 SUPPORT UNIT
SAPPER Fortification Engineering
REGIMENTAL ARMORY

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Build Hammer Heavy Axe
OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

The Sapper is a support class specializing in defensive fortifications. By default, Sappers are issued an Axe and a Hammer.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Versatile support during horde segments. The Axe boasts high damage and penetration. Fortifications give teammates time to reload and impede zombie advances.

REGIMENTAL BRANCHES

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Siege Engineer — uses Siege Engineer Armour for 66% physical and 50% explosive damage resistance, at the cost of slower building and movement.

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Keep exits clear when building so you don't accidentally trap survivors.

  • 02

    Bombers destroy clustered barricades instantly — space your defensive lines.

  • 03

    Deploy up to 30 structures per map, and 10 per individual Sapper.

  • 04

    Repair during wave intermissions. Build near Musicians for Hammer speed buffs.

WAR DISPATCH ARCHIVE

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Sappers were recruited from the strongest men in Grenadier companies and permitted to wear beards to save time on campaigns.
