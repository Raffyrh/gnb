SEAMAN

OFFENSIVE CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 004

"Defending against the past, even when it bites back."

REG // 004 ACTIVE REGIMENT
SEAMAN Naval Blunderbuss Specialist

DESCRIPTION

Seaman (a.k.a. Irregular Trooper) is an offensive class specializing in crowd control, utilizing its firearms for large amounts of close-quarters damage. Seamen are issued a Blunderbuss and a Sabre.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Blunderbuss, Sabre

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Grenadier — trades Blunderbuss for a Hand Mortar, a grenade launcher with incredibly long range and high explosive damage.

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Encouraged to be on the frontlines for close-quarters effectiveness. The Blunderbuss has high pellet count and high spread — effective against large clusters at close range but weaker at distance. Expected to prioritize Shamblers and back up against Bombers.

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    The Blunderbuss excels at clearing tight chokepoints, narrow doors, and dark corners.

  • •

    Best against hordes of Shamblers, less effective against single strong targets like the Cuirassier.

  • •

    Work directly with Musicians for reload speed buffs to minimize your downtime.

  • •

    Conserve your precious shots until Zombies are within close firing range for maximum damage.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Formerly known as Navyman. Seamen were former sailors recruited as crew-members, issued Blunderbusses for close-quarters boarding actions on ships.
