SEAMAN

OFFENSIVE CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 004

"Defending against the past, even when it bites back."

REG // 004 ACTIVE REGIMENT
SEAMAN Naval Blunderbuss Specialist
REGIMENTAL ARMORY

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Blunderbuss Sabre
OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

Seaman is an offensive class specializing in crowd control, utilizing firearms for massive close-quarters damage. Issued a Blunderbuss and Sabre.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Close-quarters spread weapon. Effective against large clusters at close range. Prioritize Shamblers and back up against dangerous Bombers.

REGIMENTAL BRANCHES

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Grenadier — trades Blunderbuss for a Hand Mortar, an explosive grenade launcher with extreme range and detonation damage.

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Excels at clearing tight chokepoints, narrow doors, and dark corners.

  • 02

    Devastating vs Shambler hordes, less effective against single armored foes.

  • 03

    Coordinate with Musicians for reload speed buffs to minimize downtime.

  • 04

    Conserve shots until enemies are point-blank for maximum pellet impact.

WAR DISPATCH ARCHIVE

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Formerly known as Navyman. Seamen were recruited sailors issued Blunderbusses for close-quarters naval boarding actions.
