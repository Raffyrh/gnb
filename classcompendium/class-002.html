OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

The Officer is an offensive class specializing in frontline support, capable of instigating high mobility and offensive buffs through its Charge ability. Officers are issued a Pistol and an Officer's Sabre.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Primary role is frontline support. The Officer's kit grants abilities that affect allies with significant offensive buffs after causing sufficient damage, plus exclusive access to Officer Callouts.

REGIMENTAL BRANCHES

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Cuirassier — grants protection buffs (Cuirass) when fighting Zombies but reduces firearm handling and walk speed.

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Infantry benefits most from Charge due to Pike and Bayonet synergy (500% damage buff).

  • 02

    Multiple Officers can chain their Charges sequentially to extend buff duration.

  • 03

    Use Charge for strategic team retreats on tight choke maps like Leipzig or Catacombes.

  • 04

    The Pistol quickly pops weak zombie types like Runners and Igniters.
