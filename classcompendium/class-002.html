OFFICER

OFFENSIVE CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 002

"A couple of chaps were cheering because they got to legally discharge their afflicted officer."

DESCRIPTION

The Officer is an offensive class specializing in frontline support, capable of instigating high mobility and offensive buffs through its Charge ability. Officers are issued a Pistol and an Officer's Sabre.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Pistol, Officer's Sabre

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Cuirassier — grants protection buffs (Cuirass) when fighting Zombies but reduces firearm handling and walk speed.

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Primary role is to provide frontline support. The Officer's kit grants abilities that affect allies with significant offensive buffs — only usable after causing sufficient damage. Has exclusive access to Officer Callouts. The Pistol compensates low damage with quickdraw and fastest reload speed.

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    Infantry benefits most from the Officer's Charge due to Pike and Bayonet synergy (500% damage buff).

  • •

    Multiple Officers can chain their Charges sequentially to extend the overall buff duration.

  • •

    Use Charge to help the team execute strategic retreats during tight choke maps like Leipzig or Catacombes de Paris.

  • •

    The Pistol is highly efficient for quickly popping weak zombie types like Runners and Igniters.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

One of the first 5 classes. Class name changes dynamically based on the selected regiment. The detailed uniform assets represents the historical rank of Captain.
