REG // 006 SUPPORT UNIT
MUSICIAN Regimental Morale & Combat Fife
REGIMENTAL ARMORY

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Fife Sabre
OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

The Musician provides team-wide buffs. One of four primary support classes alongside Sapper, Surgeon, and Chaplain. Issued a Fife and Sabre.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Click metronome on Fife to the beat to provide 20% reload speed buff and 20% build speed buff to Sappers within 50 studs. Targeted more by Runners.

REGIMENTAL BRANCHES

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Drummers and Buglers are subclasses with distinct instrument buffs (Bugle provides 100% damage boost after buildup).

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Stay behind frontlines with Fife to accelerate team reload cycles.

  • 02

    Select Drums or Bugle if taking active frontline combat roles.

  • 03

    Bugle requires kills to charge — grants a massive 100% damage boost for 20s.

  • 04

    Keep rhythm consistent to maintain unbroken aura uptime.

WAR DISPATCH ARCHIVE

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Originally named Fifer. Musicians sync automatically when playing together and historically relayed company commander signals through cannon fire.
