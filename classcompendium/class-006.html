Class Details

MUSICIAN

SUPPORT CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 006

"Just as a modern company commander relies on his radio operator, his Napoleonic counterpart depended on his drummers."

— Napoleonistyka

DESCRIPTION

The Musician is a support class specializing in providing team-wide buffs. One of four primary support classes alongside Sapper, Surgeon, and Chaplain. Musicians are issued a Fife and a Sabre.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Fife, Sabre

SUB-CLASSES & REGIMENTS

Drummers and Buglers are subclasses with different instrument-based abilities.

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Encouraged to stay just behind the frontline. Must use the metronome on their Fife by clicking to the beat of the song to provide a status effect buff within a 50-stud radius. Default buff 'Fife Boost' provides 20% Reload Speed buff and 20% build/repair speed buff to Sappers. Targeted more by Runners while playing.

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    Stay behind frontlines with Fife to help teammates reload.

  • •

    Use Drums or Bugle if planning to be on the frontlines.

  • •

    Bugle requires buildup by killing Zombies — provides 100% damage boost for 20 seconds.

  • •

    Use the class to its full potential to assist teammates.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Originally named the Fifer, changed due to addition of bagpipes. Musicians sync automatically when near another playing Musician. They acted as signalers for Company Officers — drums signaled orders over the noise of battle.
