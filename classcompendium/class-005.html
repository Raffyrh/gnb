Class Details

SURGEON

SUPPORT CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 005

"Doctors will have more lives to answer for in the next world than even we Generals."

— Napoleon Bonaparte

DESCRIPTION

The Surgeon is a support class geared around administering medical aid to allies. Lacks any firearm — only a Sabre for defense, plus Medical Supplies and a Box of Bandages.

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Bandages, Surgical Kit, Sabre

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Spawns with gauge entirely filled. Healing uses one-sixth of gauge per treatment, allowing 2 more sequences. Placing a Box of Bandages uses the entire gauge. To refill, must kill Zombies to collect supplies (1/3 chance to drop).

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • •

    Avoid frontlines unless gathering supplies or assisting wounded — stay back and tend to the wounded.

  • •

    Quick-healing technique: allow 1st phase of animation to play, jump, and repeat for faster healing.

  • •

    Have someone escort the Surgeon when gathering supplies. Vital to a team's survival — reduces casualty rates.

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

Previously had passive recharge on supplies, changed to supply drops with 1/3 spawn chance. In European armies, surgeons were considered officers due to higher education. Rank of Surgeon was equivalent to Captain in British and Prussian armies.
