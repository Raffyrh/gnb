SURGEON

SUPPORT CLASS

REGISTRY CODE // 005

"Doctors will have more lives to answer for in the next world than even we Generals."

— Napoleon Bonaparte
REG // 005 SUPPORT UNIT
SURGEON Field Medicine & Infection Halt
REGIMENTAL ARMORY

STANDARD ISSUE EQUIPMENT

Bandages Surgical Kit Sabre
OPERATIONAL PROFILE

DESCRIPTION

The Surgeon administers medical aid and halts infections. Lacks any firearm — equipped with only a Sabre for defense, plus Medical Supplies and a Box of Bandages.

COMBAT DOCTRINE

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Spawns with gauge full. Healing uses 1/6th gauge per treatment. Bandage box uses entire gauge. Refill by defeating Zombies for supplies (1/3 drop chance).

FIELD SURVIVAL DIRECTIVES

TACTICAL SURVIVAL TIPS

  • 01

    Avoid frontlines unless assisting wounded or collecting drops.

  • 02

    Quick-healing trick: jump after 1st phase of animation to heal faster.

  • 03

    Request escort when scavenging. Crucial for team survival.

WAR DISPATCH ARCHIVE

HISTORICAL TRIVIA

In European armies, surgeons held officer status due to higher education, equivalent to Captain in British and Prussian ranks.
