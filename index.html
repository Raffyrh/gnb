The Historical Apocalypse
The Burning of Moscow & The Rise of the Blight The year is 1812. Napoleon Bonaparte's Grande Armée retreats through severe Russian winter after the disastrous campaign. But they are not just running from Cossacks and cold. Deep in the mass graves of the Russo-Turkish War, a terrifying, supernatural infection has taken hold.
I.
Not a Virus, but Hell Unleashed This is no modern infection. It is a divine punishment or hellish resurgence. Fallen soldiers reanimate with single-minded malice, their souls trapped in decaying muscle and bone.
II.
30-60 Minute Reanimation Those bitten by the Blight suffer fever and excruciating delirium before turning. When the pulse stops, reanimation is swift. Comrades must execute infected allies before they turn.

Battleground Rules
Survival Demands Strict Discipline Bullets are scarce. Reload times are agonizingly long. If your team does not cooperate, the Blight will claim you one by one.
Flintlock Weaponry Muskets, carbines, and heavy rifles take up to 10 seconds to reload. Miss your shot, and you must rely on your cold sabre or bayonet to survive.
Infection Meter Every grab or bite increases your infection status. Once it hits 100%, you succumb to the Blight, immediately turning on your fellow soldiers.
Structural Defense Sappers must hammer stakes, build sturdy barricades, and secure choke points. Without defenses, endless hordes will overrun your position.
Morale & Music Musicians play historical war tunes on fife and drums, offering crucial passives including swing speed, reload boost, and damage resistance.
Assemble Your Regiment
Playable Classes & Support Roles Victory requires a balanced composition of frontline firepower, defensive engineering, and tactical medical support.
FRONTLINE MELEE & FIREPOWER Line Infantry
The backbone of any defense. Capable of firing devastating volleys and executing lethal bayonet charges.

TACTICAL LEADERSHIP Officer
Can activate 'Charge!' to boost team movement and reload speeds. Commands the defensive line.

Essential for survival. Constructs barricades, spikes, and traps to slow down the horde.

CROWD SUPPRESSION Seaman
Devastating close-range blast spread. Ideal for clearing crowded corridors and saving grabbed teammates.

Treats wounds and actively cures or halts Blight infection progression on bitten teammates.
EQUIPMENT: Bandages, Surgical Kit, Sabre

Plays historic military songs. Activates area passive buffs for nearby combatants.

Know Your Enemy
The Monstrosities of the Blight The horde is more than shambling corpses. Be prepared for tactical variants resurrected from the dead.
Shambler
LOW THREAT
Standard foot soldiers. Weak individually, but overwhelming in numbers. Headshots ensure permanent death.
Runner
MEDIUM THREAT
Fast, screaming ghouls that charge and tackle players to the ground. Must be put down instantly.
Sapper Zombie
HIGH THREAT
Armored corpses wielding heavy pickaxes. High HP; can instantly break Sapper-built barricades.
Igniter
HIGH THREAT
Zombies carrying lantern fuel. Shooting them triggers fire explosions that ignite fortifications.
Bomber
EXTREME THREAT
Holds a volatile black powder barrel. Explodes violently near players. Shoot from a distance.
Cuirassier
EXTREME THREAT
Heavily armored undead cavalry. Requires heavy musket coordination and bayonet spikes to stop.
The Theater of War
Two Brutal Paths to Survival Whether completing narrative objectives or holding the absolute final stand, survival remains highly improbable.
Objective Mode Narrative-driven levels where teams must build bridges, fire coastal cannons, escape besieged castles, or hold extraction vessels while waves of Blight crash against them.
Endless Mode No escape. Only glory. Hold a single defensive perimeter against progressively larger, faster, and more heavily armored waves of zombies. How long can your regiment stand?
